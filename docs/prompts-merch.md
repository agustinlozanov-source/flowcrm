# Prompts — fichas de producto del merch

Seis prompts completos para generar las fichas de la Tienda del portal.
Cada uno es una imagen 4:5 con un panel héroe arriba y tres detalles abajo.

**Adjunta siempre el logo claro** (`logo 2.png`), en PNG con transparencia.
Todo el merch es negro: la versión oscura sale invisible.

Flujo recomendado: genera primero el panel héroe solo, y pásalo como imagen
de referencia para los tres detalles. Evita que el producto derive entre paneles.

---

## 1 · Hoodie

```
A single composed product sheet image, divided into four panels with thin 8px dark gutters between them: one large wide hero panel across the entire top half, and three equal square panels in a row across the bottom half.

Use the attached logo image as the brand mark on the product. Reproduce it EXACTLY as provided: same shapes, same proportions, same letterforms, same spacing. Do not redraw it, do not redesign it, do not add or remove any text or element, do not change its colors beyond the material finish described. Apply it to every panel where it is visible, keeping it identical across all four panels. Wrap it realistically to the surface: it must follow the fabric folds, curvature and perspective of each shot, and pick up the same lighting, shadow and texture as the material it sits on.

TOP PANEL: a Latin American professional in their early thirties, waist-up, wearing a premium black pullover hoodie, relaxed confident posture, hands in the kangaroo pocket, looking slightly off-camera, cinematic portrait lighting. The attached logo is embroidered on the left chest, small, in white thread with visible stitch texture.
BOTTOM LEFT: the same black hoodie laid flat from directly above, sleeves folded, ribbed cuffs visible, the embroidered logo clearly visible on the left chest.
BOTTOM CENTER: extreme macro detail of the embroidered logo on the brushed cotton, raised stitching catching the light, individual thread texture visible.
BOTTOM RIGHT: back view of the same black hoodie on an invisible mannequin, hood up, the attached logo embroidered small and centered between the shoulder blades.

consistent lighting, color and branding across all four panels, studio product photography, near-black seamless background (#070708), soft key light from upper left, subtle electric blue (#0066ff) rim light, matte finish, hyperrealistic, 4k, 4:5 vertical composition
```

## Si alguno sale mal

**El logo deforme en el macro.** Es el panel que más lo estresa. Genera ese
solo, con el logo adjunto y el prompt del panel únicamente.

**Un recuadro blanco alrededor del logo.** El PNG que adjuntaste tiene fondo.
Recórtalo con transparencia real.

**El producto cambia entre paneles.** Genera el héroe primero y pásalo como
referencia para los tres de abajo. En Midjourney, `--cref`.

**Para el tema claro del portal:** cambia el fondo a `#f5f5f7`, quita el rim
light azul, y adjunta `logo 1.png` en lugar de `logo 2.png`.
